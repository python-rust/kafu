# Research — anime-style KAF fan site

## Research objective

Identify a KAF-specific visual and product direction for the first real fan-site iteration, with special attention to information architecture, art direction, and safe media acquisition.

## Primary references

### KAF Official Website

- URL: https://kaf.kamitsubaki.jp/
- About: https://kaf.kamitsubaki.jp/about/
- Observed product structure: SCHEDULE, NEWS, DISCOGRAPHY, MOVIE, ABOUT/social destinations.
- Design implication: the artist identity and current works should be visible quickly; the fan site should not try to mirror all official operational content.

### KAF 3rd Anniversary special site

- URL: https://kaf3rdanniversary.kamitsubaki.jp/
- History: https://kaf3rdanniversary.kamitsubaki.jp/history/
- Observed pattern: oversized key visual, sparse editorial copy, chronological storytelling/history, image-led presentation.
- Design implication: special-site storytelling is a better reference for a fan experience than copying the operational structure of the current official home page.

### KAMITSUBAKI CITY UNDER CONSTRUCTION anime site

- URL: https://kamitsubaki-anime.jp/
- Observed pattern: strong key visual, story/worldbuilding framing, character-focused imagery, section navigation, music/special content, high-contrast graphic atmosphere.
- Design implication: borrow the confidence of worldbuilding and image scale, but avoid turning a KAF artist fan site into an anime-series portal.

### Hatsune Miku 10th Anniversary special site

- URL: https://piapro.net/miku10th/
- Observed pattern: large celebratory illustration, visual/history/gallery sections, content organized around the character/artist visual rather than application-like UI chrome.
- Design implication: an illustration-led archive/gallery is an appropriate secondary reference when KAF-specific special-site patterns do not cover a component need.

## Rights / media references

### KAMITSUBAKI secondary-creation guidelines

- URL: https://kamitsubaki.jp/guidelines/
- Relevant finding: individual non-commercial fan secondary creation is permitted under stated conditions and must not be presented as official.
- Important limitation: this is not blanket permission to copy every official website image, logo, campaign asset, or third-party artwork into a repository.

### KAF on piapro

- URL: https://piapro.jp/characters/kafu
- Relevant finding: KAF is a supported character with user-posted illustrations available on piapro.
- Asset-use implication: each work's actual usage/license conditions still need to be checked before download and reuse.

### piapro work license conditions

- URL: https://piapro.jp/license/pc/icon
- Intro / usage explanation: https://piapro.jp/intro/
- Relevant finding: works can specify non-commercial-only, no-modification, attribution, or custom license conditions; assets without a compatible license require separate permission.

### Example of an explicitly restricted KAF asset

- URL: https://kaf.kamitsubaki.jp/schedule/20260527/1206/
- Relevant finding: the 5th-album download campaign explicitly prohibits reproduction, redistribution, and commercial use of the campaign bonus wallpaper.
- Design implication: never infer reuse permission from the fact that an image is downloadable or publicly promoted.

## Product synthesis

The strongest common pattern is **art first, editorial hierarchy second, UI chrome third**.

For KAF specifically, a warm editorial base with ink-like structure and rose/magenta accents is more authentic than defaulting to generic dark neon/cyberpunk anime styling. The existing project name `KAF Observatory` is useful because observation/archive language naturally supports issue numbers, metadata, selected works, and a curated visual archive without impersonating the official site.

## Chosen MVP concept

`KAF Editorial Observatory`

- Single-page curated fan publication.
- One strong rights-cleared hero visual.
- Fan-written profile and context.
- Four or more selected works rather than a full discography mirror.
- Small rights-cleared visual archive with explicit attribution.
- Official destinations kept prominent and clearly external.
- No dynamic scraping, no audio redistribution, no Live2D, no generative character art.

## Reference use rule

References are for hierarchy, pacing, composition, and product structure. Do not reproduce layouts, logos, illustrations, or protected graphic assets one-to-one.
