# Visual and product direction research

## Research objective

Identify a KAF-specific, high-quality anime / virtual-singer direction that supports a staged career journey and avoids both the current paper-editorial treatment and generic neon UI styling.

Research date: 2026-08-25.

## Primary official references

### KAF 3rd Anniversary — History

- Source: https://kaf3rdanniversary.kamitsubaki.jp/history/
- The page organizes KAF's development by year and dated events, repeatedly pairing milestones with imagery.
- Key milestones include the 2019 first one-man live and first album `観測`, the 2020 `魔法` period, the 2021 `REBUILDING` live, and V.W.P formation.
- Product implication: a chronological journey is native to KAF's existing narrative, not an invented UI gimmick.

### KAF 5th Album `深愛` special site

- Source: https://kaf.kamitsubaki.jp/transcendent-love/
- The site is image-dense and chapter-like, moving through concept, artist/creator messages, products, story material, and track-by-track imagery.
- It frames the album as an intersection of music, story, and visual material.
- Product implication: current KAF presentation supports layered worldbuilding and a more immersive visual density than the existing fan homepage.

### KAF 5th ONE-MAN LIVE `宿声 / 深愛`

- Source: https://yokohamawars2026.kamitsubaki.jp/kaf
- The current live presentation uses loading/entry atmosphere, a dominant key visual, and a vertically sequenced special-site structure.
- The connected `深愛` material describes a record/world born from one voice and ties current work to a broader narrative.
- Product implication: the final chapter should feel like a current culmination rather than another equal-weight list item.

### TV anime `KAMITSUBAKI CITY UNDER CONSTRUCTION`

- Source: https://kamitsubaki-anime.jp/
- The official anime site prioritizes worldbuilding, large key visuals, character identity, and image-led section navigation.
- Product implication: borrow its confidence in character scale and atmosphere, but do not turn the fan site into an anime-series portal or copy its layout.

## Synthesis

The common pattern is:

1. character / artwork first;
2. world or chapter framing second;
3. concise explanatory copy third;
4. navigation and metadata last.

The redesign should therefore move from `KAF Editorial Observatory` to `KAF Phenomenon Chapters`: a dark, luminous, chapter-based journey with strong visual changes per era.

## Six-chapter narrative recommendation

1. **2018 — Origin / Discovery**
2. **2019 — Observation**
3. **2020–2021 — Magic / Rebuilding**
4. **2022–2023 — Expansion**
5. **2024 — Fable / Second Chapter**
6. **2025–2026 — Transcendent Love**

The content task must verify every milestone against current official artist, discography, live, or announcement pages. This research sets the narrative grouping, not a license to copy official wording.

## Reference-use rule

Use these sites for composition, scale, pacing, hierarchy, and atmosphere. Do not reproduce their logos, CSS, image assets, layouts, or copy one-to-one.
